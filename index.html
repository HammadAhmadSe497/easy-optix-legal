Privacy Policy — Easy Optix

Last updated: October 2026
Operator: Basecircuit
Contact: support@basecircuit.com

Easy Optix is a shop management app for optical retailers. This policy explains what we collect and why.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: owner/staff name, Pakistani mobile number (03XXXXXXXXX), and password (stored hashed on our servers; the app does not keep the password).
  • Shop business data you enter: shop name, address, branding/logo, payment QR images, invoices, advances, catalog prices, customer names and phone numbers, and prescription values on invoices.
  • Diagnostics: app version and basic error/network information needed to run sync.
  • Subscription payment proof: the transaction ID you type after paying outside the app. We do not collect card numbers through Google Play.

2. How we use it

To create your account, run the shop, sync between devices, confirm subscriptions, provide support, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data. We do not show ads.

3. Where it is stored

On the device (local database / encrypted backup file) and on our cloud. Sync uses HTTPS.

4. Sharing

We use infrastructure providers (hosting/database) only to operate the service. We do not share shop data with advertisers. We may disclose data if required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep data while the shop account is active. You can export a local backup from Data Backup in the app. To delete a shop account and cloud copies, email support@basecircuit.com from the registered owner contact. Uninstalling the app does not wipe the cloud copy.

6. Children

Easy Optix is for business users and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted in the app Terms.
