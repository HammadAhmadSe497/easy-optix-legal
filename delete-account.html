Delete your Easy Optix account

Operator: Basecircuit

Easy Optix does not have an in-app account deletion button. To delete a shop account and the associated cloud data, email us from the registered owner contact.

Send a message to support@basecircuit.com with:

  • Subject: Easy Optix account deletion
  • The shop name
  • The owner mobile number used to sign in (03XXXXXXXXX)
  • A clear request to delete the account and cloud copies

We will confirm the request and delete the account and associated cloud data. Uninstalling the app from a phone does not wipe the cloud copy.
